a) Macroscopic

characteristic; taste pungent, aromatic, and followed by a sensation of cold. Readily
pulverisable in the presence of a little alcohol (95 per cent), chloroform, or solvent ether.

ASSAY
Karp£ra contains not less than 96.0 per cent of Camphor (C10 H16O), when analysed as below:
Weigh accurately about 0.2 g and dissolve in 25 ml of aldehyde-free alcohol in a 300
ml flask. Slowly add while stirring 75 ml of dinitrophenylhydrazine solution and heat on a
water bath for four hours under reflux. Remove alcohol by distillation, allow to cool, dilute
to 200 ml with a 2 per cent v/v solution of sulphuric acid. Set aside for twentyfour hours,
filter in tared Gooch crucible, and wash the precipitate with successive quantities of 10 ml of
cold water until the washings are neutral to litmus paper. Dry to constant weight at 800 and
weigh.
Each g of precipitate is equivalent to 0.458 g of C10 H16O.
