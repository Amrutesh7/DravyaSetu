DESCRIPTION
a) Macroscopic
It is light yellow to reddish brown solid, blocks or spherical solid forms or in the form
of coarse granules with pleasant and characteristic odour. It does not show the presence of
insects, vegetable debris or fibres when examined with naked eyes in daylight.
