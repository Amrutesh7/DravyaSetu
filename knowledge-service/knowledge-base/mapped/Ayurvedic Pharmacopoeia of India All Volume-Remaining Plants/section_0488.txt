DESCRIPTION
a) Macroscopic
Roots cylindrical, in cut pieces upto 10 cm. long and 1.5 cm. thick; surface pale
brown with longitudinal wrinkles and scars of rootlets; fracture, short and horny; odour and
taste indistinct.
b) Microscopic
Root shows narrow cork consisting of rectangular and tangentially elongated cells;
phellogen single layered, phelloderm consists of tangentially elongated parenchyma cells,

elements are thin walled and non-lignified.
Powder -Powder shows thin walled parenchyma cells, some of them containing rosette of
