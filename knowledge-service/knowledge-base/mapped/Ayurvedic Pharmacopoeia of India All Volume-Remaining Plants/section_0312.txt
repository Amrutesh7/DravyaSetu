DESCRIPTION
a) Macroscopic
Seeds hard, trigonous, having short wings; size 0.5 to 1.0 cm long and 0.3 to 0.5 cm
wide; colour greyish-cream; odour, not characteristic; taste; slightly bitter.

1

b) Microscopic
Seed shows 10 to 15 layered, tangentially elongated, thin-walled cells of the testa,
followed by a wide zone of cells of cotyledons consisting of round to oval, thin-walled,
parenchymatous cells with intercellular spaces and containing mucilage and oil globules.
Powder- Cream coloured; shows groups of elongated, round to oval, parenchymatous cells;
oval to elongated, thin-walled cells of testa showing striations in surface view and oil
globules.
