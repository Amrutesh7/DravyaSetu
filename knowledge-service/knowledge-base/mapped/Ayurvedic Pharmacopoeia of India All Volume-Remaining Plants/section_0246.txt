DESCRIPTION
a) Macroscopic
Drug consists of pieces of wood of variable lengths and widths, brown, very hard and
strong; close-grained, annual ring not distinct, rays fine, pores uniformly distributed joined
by wavy concentric bands; fracture hard and tough.

 285

b) Microscopic
Heart wood shows well developed xylem, consisting of usual elements, vessels
simple pitted, solitary or 2-3 in groups, arranged in radial rings, a few contain reddish-
brown content; parenchyma thick walled and paratracheal; medullary rays 1-3 cells wide;
fibres abundant in numbers and present in groups alternating with the bands of xylem
parenchyma.
Powder - Brown; under microscope shows fibres, tracheids and parenchymatous cells.
