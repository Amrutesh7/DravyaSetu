DESCRIPTION
a) Macroscopic
Seeds small, slightly oblong, pale or reddish-brown, bright, smooth, 1.2- 1.5 mm in
dia.; under magnifying glass it is seen to be minutely reticulated; taste, bitter and sharp.

 279

b) Microscopic
Seed shows single layered colourless testa followed by 3-5 layered, non-lignified,
hexagonal, thick-walled cells filled with yellowish-brown contents; embryo and endosperm
consists of hexagonal, thin-walled parenchymatous cells containing oil globules.
Powder - Yellow in colour with brown particles and oily, slightly bitter and sharp in taste;
shows frequently thick-walled, fragments of reddish-brown cells of hypodermis, yellowish
hyaline masses.
