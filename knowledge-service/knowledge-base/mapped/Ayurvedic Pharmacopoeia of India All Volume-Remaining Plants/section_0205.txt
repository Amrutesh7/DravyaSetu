DESCRIPTION
a) Macroscopic
Flower white, solitary, 10-13 cm across; sepals 4, outside greenish to brownish,
inside whitish; petals about 10, white; stamens many, outer ones being transformed
successively from petals; anthers linear small without appendages; pistil syncarpous, carpels
10-16, sunk in fleshy disk, ovary multicellular and crowned by a large stigma with 16 rays,
each with a cylindrical appendages, ovules many, fruit a berry.
 154

b) Microscopic
Powder - Light-brown; shows polygonal, thin-walled epidermal cells in surface
view, stellate hairs and spherical or trigonal pollen grains, measuring 11-24 µ in dia.
