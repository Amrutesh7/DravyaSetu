DESCRIPTION
a) Macroscopic
Rhizome- horizontal and unbranched, spindle shaped, 12 to 20 cm long and dull white
to creamy in colour when fresh, prominently marked with nodal rings and scale leaves which
completely encircle the nodes; internodal length is 0.5 to 1.5 cm; sliced individual pieces are
cylindrical, rough, and size ranges from 1 to 2 cm long and 1 to 2.5 cm across; externally
brownish and broken surface off-white; fracture, hard and fractured surface fibrous and
starchy; starchy odour and taste.
b) Microscopic
