DESCRIPTION
a) Macroscopic
Seed oblong, hard, woody, 2-5 cm long and 1.5-1.7 cm wide; surface wrinkled, light
brown to black; two sharp recurved hooks present at anterior end; four prominent grooves
present each on convex and concave side and on lateral sides, 2-4 hairy spines present
inside groove on concave side; no taste and odour.
 120

b) Microscopic
Powder- Black and rough; shows groups of thick-walled cells, numerous fibres,
unicellular hairs and oil globules.
