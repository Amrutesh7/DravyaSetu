DESCRIPTION
a) Macroscopic
Fruit pulp occurs as a reddish-brown, moist, sticky mass, in which yellowish-brown
fibres are readily seen; odour, pleasant; taste, sweetish and acidic.

3

b) Microscopic
Fruit pulp consists of thin-walled, elongated to polygonal, parenchymatous cells of
considerable size, traversed by a number of long fibro-vascular bundles and having a very
few small starch granules, and numerous prismatic crystals of calcium oxalate.
