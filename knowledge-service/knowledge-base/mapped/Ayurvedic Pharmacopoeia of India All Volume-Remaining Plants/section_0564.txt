ASSAY
It contains not less than 85per cent, w/v of phenolic substances, chiefly eugenol, C10H12O2
when analysed as follows:
Pipette 10 ml of clove oil in a Cassia flask, the neck of which is graduated from 0 to 6 ml at
intervals of 0.1 ml. Add 75 ml of potassium hydroxide solution. Shake the mixture for five
min. and heat for ten min. in boiling water, shaking the flask at least three times during
heating. Cool to room temperature and when liquids have completely separated, add
sufficient potassium hydroxide solution to raise the lower level of the oily layer with in the
graduated portion of the flask. Keep aside for 18 hours and read the volume of oily layer. Not
more than 1.5 ml of oil separates indicating the presence of not less than 85 per cent of w/v
of total eugenol.
