DESCRIPTION
a) Macroscopic
Seed stony, very hard, spherical, obovoid or oval, variable in size, about 1 or 2 cm in
dia.; longitudinally grooved, tubercled, brown, divided into five segments.

1

b) Microscopic
Seed coat consists of multilayered, oval to polygonal stone cells and internally
followed by 8 to 10 layers of tangentially elongated, oval-shaped, thin-walled,
parenchymatous cells, filled with reddish-brown contents, excepting the middle 2 or 3
layers; endosperm consists of oval to polygonal, thin-walled, parenchymatous cells; rosette
crystals of calcium oxalate and oil globules present in this region; embryo slightly curved
and consists of oval to polygonal, thin-walled, parenchymatous cells, a few having oil
globules.
Powder - Reddish-brown; shows polygonal lignified with narrow lumened stone cells, thin-
walled, parenchymatous cells with reddish-brown contents, rosette crystals of calcium
oxalate and oil globules.
